TORNPULSE v1.1.0 — TRAVEL AGENCY HUB

WHAT CHANGED
- Chat is not present in the running TornPulse app.
- Added a Travel Agency card to the main TornPulse dashboard.
- Detects an active Torn flight through the read-only Torn API.
- Shows origin, destination, live countdown, local arrival time, and Torn arrival time.
- Automatically schedules an Android notification 5 minutes before landing.
- Automatically schedules another Android notification when the flight lands.
- Includes a button to open Torn's official Travel Agency for player-controlled travel.
- Keeps the existing Item Market and all HUD features.
- Bumped app version to 1.1.0 and Android versionCode to 25.

BUILD NOTES
- Existing legacy patches safely skip when the newer Item Market / Travel source is present.
- patch-v115-travel-agency.cjs is idempotent and exits successfully after installation.
- The app refreshes its Torn snapshot when opened and every two minutes while active.
- Scheduled landing alerts can fire while TornPulse is closed, provided Android notification permission remains enabled.
- Flight detection first uses Torn's user/travel response and falls back to the profile status/arrival timestamp.
